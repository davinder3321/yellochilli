## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal warmth, master-chef culinary authority, and contemporary luxury of modern Indian haute cuisine. Built specifically for guest-facing touchpoints, reservation flows, and post-dining review engagement, the aesthetic pairs culinary indulgence with effortless, frictionless utility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Warm Culinary Opulence:** Evoking the aromatic richness of saffron, warm whole spices, and copper accents, shifting away from generic sterile layouts into an inviting, hospitable world.
- **Mastery & Trust:** Rooted in the celebrated reputation of Master Chef Sanjeev Kapoor, projecting prestige, culinary innovation, and uncompromised hospitality standards.
- **Modern Refinement:** Clean, generous negative space balanced against rich cream surfaces, gossamer gold borders, and deep charcoal typographic precision.

### Design Movement & Visual Style
The system adopts an **Editorial Neo-Luxury** style. It blends classical high-contrast typography and subtle metallic rim-lighting with tactile interactive micro-surfaces. Card components feel physical and reassuring through warm ambient shadows, while buttons and review hubs deliver instant clarity and high actionability.

## Colors

The palette balances culinary vibrancy with luxury neutrality:

- **Primary Saffron Gold (`#D97706` / `#E59819`):** Represents rich spices, tandoori warmth, and golden finishes. Used for primary interactive accents, focus rings, badges, and key ratings.
- **Secondary Chili Crimson (`#B91C1C` / `#991B1B`):** Honors the brand's namesake yellow chilli and fiery spice palette. Applied to promotional highlights, callouts, and key conversion moments.
- **Tertiary & Background Charcoal (`#111827` / `#1F2937`):** Deep ebony-charcoal providing grounding typography and dark luxury footer backgrounds, ensuring readable contrast against light tones.
- **Neutral Ivory & Cream (`#FFFDF7` / `#FEF9EE`):** Warm, inviting canvas backgrounds replacing stark hospital whites with delicate, linen-like comfort.
- **Accent Metallics & Feedback (`border-gold`, `star-gold`):** Gossamer hairline borders and warm amber stars that lend fine-dining restraint. Platform-native colors for Google (`#4285F4`) and Yelp (`#D32323`) are preserved within dedicated social action components.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the geometry and readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Playfair Display introduces a culinary magazine feel, featuring italic flourishes on subheadings and high-impact title weights for dish descriptions and chef credentials.
- **Body & Operational Copy:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures instant legibility across ambient restaurant lighting conditions, small phone screens, and high-density menu or review cards.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows (`label-caps`):** Rendered uppercase with generous tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce section markers such as "SIGNATURE DISHES", "GUEST EXPERIENCES", and "DANVILLE, CA".

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a responsive fluid grid that prioritizes table service touchpoints, digital menus, and quick-tap review workflows.

- **Desktop & Tablets:** 12-column layout with max-width containment of `1200px` for editorial pages and `840px` for transactional flows (e.g., reservation booking, review routing). 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters ensure cards and visual assets breathe comfortably.
- **Mobile & In-Venue QR Hub:** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) lateral margins. Review cards, action buttons, and rating stars are sized for comfortable single-thumb operation (minimum tap target: 48px).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows multiples of 4px/8px, where internal card padding uses `space-md` (mobile) to `space-lg` (desktop), while separation between narrative sections utilizes `space-xl` and extended margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, tinted illumination rather than stark gray drop shadows, simulating soft candlelit restaurant ambiance:

- **Level 1 (Subtle In-Flow Containers):** `0 1px 3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.05)` coupled with a `1px` subtle golden outline (`border-gold-subtle`).
- **Level 2 (Tactile Review & Menu Cards):** `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.04)`. Imparts lift and paper-like presence.
- **Level 3 (Modals, QR Overlays & Floating Actions):** `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.16), 0 1px 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Card Edge Details:** High-tier cards feature a fine 1px golden hair-line border (`#F3E1B9`) that catches subtle viewport light against the warm ivory canvas.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:
- Standard UI controls, inputs, and feedback buttons use `0.5rem` (8px) border radius.
- Review cards, featured recipe highlights, and QR container panels feature `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Badges, star rating chips, and platform pill tags apply full-pill radius (`9999px`) to maintain approachable, tactile interactions.
- Avoid razor-sharp edges; soften all bounding boxes to match the warmth of hospitality.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Dining CTA:** Solid saffron gold (`#D97706`) with white text, transitioning to `#B45309` on hover. Padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, rounded-md, with a soft gold focus glow.
- **Secondary Culinary Action:** Warm ivory fill with a crisp `1px` border of `#D97706` and text in `#B45309`.
- **Platform Review CTAs (Google & Yelp):**
  - *Google Button:* White surface, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, full-color Google 'G' icon, bold charcoal text (`#1F2937`).
  - *Yelp Button:* Yelp-red fill (`#D32323`), crisp white typography, branded Yelp burst icon.
  - Both platform buttons are minimum 52px tall on mobile review hubs for effortless one-touch tapping.

### Tactile Review Cards
- White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with `1rem` radius, 1px subtle gold perimeter border (`#F3E1B9`), and Level 2 ambient shadow.
- Top row: 5-star rating using `#F59E0B` filled stars alongside a verified diner chip.
- Middle: Editorial quote rendered in `Playfair Display` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` with rich charcoal text (`#1F2937`).
- Bottom row: Reviewer initials inside a saffron circular avatar, reviewer name, and visit date in muted tone (`#4B5563`).

### QR Code Mobile Review Hub
- Focused, single-column viewport centered on mobile screens.
- Header featuring Master Chef endorsement crest, a warm headline ("Share Your Dining Experience"), and a 5-star interactive prompt.
- Dual primary action buttons: "Review us on Google" and "Review us on Yelp", accompanied by a subtle direct feedback option for manager attention.
- High-contrast QR display container framed with delicate gold corners for in-table standees.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#FEF9EE`, 1px border in `#E5E7EB`.
- Active / Focus: Border changes to `#D97706` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.20)`. No default browser outlines.
- Placeholder text in `#9CA3AF`, label in `label-md` with `#1F2937`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Height: 28px; pill-shaped.
- Vegetarian / Signature Dishes: Warm ivory background, `#B45309` border, gold chili or leaf micro-icon.
- Star Rating Chip: Filled pill with `#FEF9EE` background, single gold star icon, and bold rating text (e.g., "4.9 ★ (350+ Reviews)").
